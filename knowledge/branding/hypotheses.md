# Branding hypotheses

- A face reads as "this is about you zoning out", which fits the product better than an eye ("being watched").
- A black face on the yellow squircle stays legible in both light and dark Chrome toolbars.
- Small rotations (a tilted logo, a -1.5° selected segment and outer counts, a -2° pill) read as playful "woozy" without hurting legibility. Watch for user feedback that it looks broken.
